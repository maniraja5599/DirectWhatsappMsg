FiFTO Direct App

Privacy Policy — Last updated: September 10, 2026

In short: FiFTO Direct App does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Information We Collect

FiFTO Direct App does not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account required — The app works without sign-up or login.
  • No data sent to servers — Phone numbers, messages, and images you enter are processed entirely on your device and never transmitted to our servers.
  • No analytics or tracking — We do not use any analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.
  • No location data — The app does not access your location.
  • No contacts access — The app does not read or access your contacts.

Local Storage

The app uses your browser's local storage to save:

  • Saved messages (stored only on your device)
  • Theme preference (light/dark mode)
  • WhatsApp mode setting
  • Recent phone numbers
  • Draft messages

This data never leaves your device and is automatically cleared if you clear your browser data or uninstall the app.

Third-Party Services

The app redirects you to WhatsApp (or WhatsApp Business) via deep links. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies when you use their service. We do not have any integration with WhatsApp's API or servers.

Clipboard

The app may read your clipboard to paste phone numbers (only when you grant permission). Clipboard data is not stored or transmitted anywhere.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
